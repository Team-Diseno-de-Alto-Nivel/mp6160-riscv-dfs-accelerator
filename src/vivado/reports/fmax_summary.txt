--- Note for issue #67 (on-board bring-up) ---
204 MHz is the Fmax reported for #66 (the maximum this build demonstrably
closes at), but it is NOT the recommended operating frequency for the
actual bitstream used on the KV260. Reasons:
  - the margin at 204 MHz is razor-thin (see above) and #67's bitstream
    comes from its own synth/impl run, which can place/route slightly
    differently and eat that margin;
  - on real hardware a timing violation doesn't show up as a report line,
    it shows up as silent wrong answers or intermittent behavior, which is
    much harder to debug than rereading a timing report.
Set PSU__CRL_APB__PL0_REF_CTRL__FREQMHZ to 200 (not 204) for #67's build,
for real margin at a negligible (~2%) speed cost.
